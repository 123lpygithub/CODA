Rubric:
- clarity: 1-5, how clearly and consistently the codebook definitions can be understood.
- category_boundaries: 1-5, how clearly definitions and inclusion/exclusion conditions distinguish categories.
- meaningful_coverage: 1-5, how well the codebook covers relevant phenomena and important variation.
- evidence_support: 0-2 for one artifact-code assignment: 0 unsupported/contradicted, 1 partly supported, 2 fully supported without unsupported technical or causal claims.
- If evidence cannot be judged, use evidence_support status `unassessable` and omit score.

Codebook anchors (2 and 4 lie between the adjacent anchors):
- Clarity 1: vague or inconsistent definitions prevent reliable interpretation;
  3: most definitions are understandable but some require substantial clarification;
  5: labels and definitions are precise, consistent, and understandable in context.
- Boundaries 1: categories repeatedly duplicate or conflate the same interpretation;
  3: main distinctions are usable but some boundary cases remain ambiguous;
  5: definitions and inclusion/exclusion conditions support clear distinctions,
  including legitimate multiple coding.
- Coverage 1: major relevant phenomena are absent or obscured by overly broad categories;
  3: common phenomena are represented but relevant distinctions or less frequent
  phenomena are missed; 5: categories represent relevant phenomena and important
  variations found in the inspected material.
